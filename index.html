Welcome Message

A School That Shapes Character as Much as Careers

Greenfield Academy stands as one of Johannesburg's most respected independent schools — a place where academic rigour meets genuine care for every learner who walks through our gates. Since 1948, we have guided thousands of young minds toward their fullest potential.

Our ethos is built on four pillars: intellectual curiosity, moral courage, physical vitality and service to community. These values do not simply appear in our prospectus — they live in the daily decisions our learners make, the relationships they build and the leaders they become.

Whether your child is drawn to science and mathematics, the arts and culture, or the sporting field, Greenfield provides the environment, the mentorship and the opportunity to flourish.

JM
Mr Jonathan Mokoena
Headmaster, Greenfield Academy
